# Riverside donor tools

A small project for DVA's work with Riverside Animal Rescue: donor data checks, refund handling and the web form's cursor code. It's also Lighthouse's probe fixture, so keep it realistic and keep real client data out of it.

- The lead is named in `docs/STATUS.md`. Take decisions and blockers to the lead with `wr`.
- Decisions are in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Other teams' work in flight is in `docs/handoffs/`.
- Small PRs. End each PR body with `Lighthouse-Session: <your $WORKROOM_SESSION_ID>`.
- Slack routes are listed in `docs/slack-routes.md`.
